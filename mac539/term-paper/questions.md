## Edelman Trust Barometer

> 앞 질문이 X축, competence & 뒤 질문이 Y축, ethics; 순서대로 1~7점 & 1~11점 응답 데이터

To what extent do you agree with the following statement? {Trustee} in general is good at what it does

> Trustee: Government, Media, Business, NGOs, Media Brands
> 긍정 응답 비율(상위 3개 척도)에서 부정 응답 비율(하위 3개 척도)을 뺀 순점수(Net score)를 X축 좌표로 사용

In thinking about why you do or do not trust {Trustee}, please specify where you think they fall on the scale between the two opposing descriptions.

Purpose-Driven: "Highly effective agents of positive change" vs "Completely ineffective agents of positive change"

Honest: "Honest and fair" vs "Corrupt and biased"

Vision: "Have a vision for the future that I believe in" vs "Do not have a vision for the future that I believe in"

Fairness: "Serve the interests of everyone equally and fairly" vs "Serve the interests of only certain groups of people"

> 각 4개의 차원마다 긍정 평가 비율(상위 5개 척도)에서 부정 평가 비율(하위 5개 척도)을 뺀 순점수(Net score)를 구한 뒤, 이 4개 점수의 평균을 내어 Y축 좌표로 사용

> 본 연구의 경우에는 온도 0.0의 단일모델 응답결과이니 바로 단순 선형변환 (1점: -100, 6점: 0, 11점: 100)

### Football Manager 2026 five star coaches
As always, here you will find the most comprehensive list of *FM 2026 best staff*, including **hundreds of coaches for all training areas**.
*Date of first publication*: Oct 26th, 2025
#### Criteria for FM26 best coaches in this list
The list include real coaches (no regens) with current star rating of at least 4 stars (out of 5) for any given coaching category, except fitness coaches and goalkeeping coaches. The minimum rating for these areas is 4.5 stars because there are too many of those in the game.
I have included staff who could have interest in a coach position (general, fitness or goalkeeping), but their main staff role (or current job if employed) might be something else. Refer to the corresponding column to check on their main or current role.
Using this list with help you sign the **best coaches in FM26**, some of them will be without contract and you can offer them a contract at once.
#### Coaches have potential to improve too
Since the star rating is current, it's only natural some of them will improve their ratings as seasons go by in-game and new ones will emerge (either regens or former players who become coaches after retiring).
#### Rule of Thumb: One Coach Per Training Area
Remember that to *get full stars* from each coach, you need to assign him on just one training area at a time. This means that you need 2 fitness coaches, 1 for strength training and 1 for aerobic training. It also means you need 2 goalkeeping coaches, 1 for shot stopping and 1 for handling & distribution.

This list includes some of the *most notable free agents in Football Manager 26* who would be willing to join a team from the top divisions; mainly top flight sides or second division ones in certain nations, such as English Championship. So essentially these are the **best FM 2026 players available on a free transfer** at the beginning of the first season (July 7th, 2025).
More than 350 of the *top FM26 free agents* are included.
Date of first publication: 3 October 2024  
**Updated:** 29 October 2025
This is the successor of my ultimate list of [Football Manager 2024 best free agents](a-football-manager-2024-best-free-players.html).
### What are free agents?
They are *players without contract* available on a free transfer. Free players are ideal to boost your squad when your transfer budget is very limited. They are usually players in the twilight of their career, but there are always cases of younger players who are still free for different reasons.
You can quickly see which players are injury prone; they have a relevant icon next to their names.
### What does the rating mean?
It's my personal recommendation system for your convenience. I'm using a 1-100 scale and the ratings are based on English divisions.
For the first time, you will see two ratings; R reflects the player's quality at current ability, while R+ is an estimate of the player's potential growth. This estimate considers the difference between CA and PA, their age and their relevant attributes that affect development.
**80+** key player for 1st division teams  
**77-79** rotation/backup for 1st division teams  
**74-76** important player for 2nd division teams  
**71-73** important player for 3rd division teams

# FM26: The Ultimate Guide to Building a Winning Tactic

Want to dominate in Football Manager 2026? Building a successful tactic is crucial, and with the new IP/OOP role system, understanding the fundamentals is more important than ever. This guide will walk you through the process of creating a tactic that suits your squad and playing style, covering everything from formation selection to player roles and team instructions. You'll learn how to leverage the new FM26 mechanics to craft a truly winning strategy.
**Key Takeaways:**
* FM26 introduces separate In Possession (IP) and Out of Possession (OOP) roles.
* Your IP formation dictates your attacking shape, while your OOP formation determines your defensive setup.
* Team instructions fine-tune your tactical approach in both phases of play.
## Step-by-Step: Crafting Your FM26 Masterpiece
Building a tactic in FM26 is a structured process. Here's a step-by-step guide to get you started:
1. **Choose your IP Formation:** This is your attacking blueprint. Do you want to play with a 4-3-3, a 4-2-3-1, or something more unconventional? Consider your squad's strengths. If you have pacey wingers, a 4-3-3 might be ideal. If you have a creative central attacking midfielder, a 4-2-3-1 could be a better fit.
2. **Choose your OOP Formation:** This is your defensive shape. It can be the same as your IP formation or completely different. For example, you might attack in a 4-3-3 but defend in a more compact 4-1-4-1. Think about how you want to defend – high press, mid block, or low block (more on these later).
3. **Assign IP Roles:** Now, assign IP roles to each player. This determines what they do when you have the ball. A Full-Back (IP) could be an Inverted Wing-Back, stepping into midfield to offer passing options, or a more traditional Wing-Back, providing width. A striker could be a Central Forward, leading the line, or a more creative Deep-Lying Forward, dropping deeper to link play.
4. **Assign OOP Roles:** Next, assign OOP roles to each player. This dictates their defensive responsibilities. A player's OOP role doesn't have to match their IP role. For example, a player might be a Channel Midfielder (IP), making forward runs in attack, but a Wide-Covering CM (OOP), tracking back to protect the flanks when defending.
5. **Set IP Team Instructions:** These instructions dictate your attacking style. Do you want to play through the press with short passes, or bypass it with long balls? Adjust your Passing Directness, Tempo, Width, and Creative Freedom accordingly.
6. **Set OOP Team Instructions:** These instructions dictate your defensive approach. Choose your pressing intensity (High Press, Mid Block, or Low Block) and set pressing traps to direct the opposition into specific areas.
7. **Check the Visualiser:** The Visualiser is your best friend. Use it to see how your team will position in both phases of play. Pay attention to any areas where your players are too close together or too far apart. Your staff will also flag any impractical role combinations.
## Mastering the Art of Player Roles: IP vs. OOP
The beauty of FM26 lies in the flexibility of the IP/OOP system. It allows you to create highly specialized roles for each player, maximizing their impact in both phases of play.
* **IP Roles:** These roles govern a player's movement and responsibilities when your team has possession. Think about the runs they make, the passes they attempt, and the areas of the pitch they occupy.
* **OOP Roles:** These roles determine a player's defensive positioning, pressing intensity, and tackling style. Think about how they contribute to your defensive shape and how they win the ball back.
**Example:** A central midfielder could be a *Channel Midfielder (IP)*, making late runs into the box to support the attack, and a *Wide-Covering CM (OOP)*, tracking back to cover the flanks when out of possession. This gives you attacking dynamism and defensive solidity in one player.
### Combining Roles for Tactical Flexibility
This is where the magic happens. By carefully combining IP and OOP roles, you can create a team that is both dangerous in attack and difficult to break down in defence.
### Understanding Role Compatibility
While the system is flexible, some roles naturally complement each other better than others. Pay attention to player attributes and preferred moves when assigning roles.
## Building a Giant-Killing Tactic: FM26 Underdog Strategies
Want to upset the odds and conquer the giants of football? A well-designed underdog tactic can be your secret weapon. Here's how to build one in FM26:
1. **Core Principle:** Low Block (OOP) + Fast Counters (IP)
2. **OOP Formation:** 4-1-4-1 or 5-3-2 – compact, narrow, and difficult to penetrate.
3. **IP Formation:** 4-3-3 or 4-2-3-1 – quick transitions with wide outlets to exploit space.
**Key OOP Roles:**
* 2x Covering CB – sweep up through balls and cover for teammates.
* Holding Full-Back – stay back and defend, don't get caught out of position.
* Screening DM – protect the back four and break up opposition attacks.
* Tracking Winger – drop back and help defend in wide areas.
* Central Outlet CF – stay high up the pitch, ready for the counter-attack.
**Key IP Roles:**
* Channel Forward or Wide Forward – exploit space left by the opposition pushing up.
* Channel Midfielder – make diagonal runs to create overloads quickly.
* Ball-Playing CB – play out from the back quickly when possession is won.
**Team Instructions:**
* OOP: Low Block, sit deep, no aggressive pressing.
* IP: Bypass Press (go direct quickly), high Directness, use flank/channel runners.
**In-Match Adjustments:**
* When protecting a lead, switch IP to even more direct and defensive.
* When you need a goal, switch OOP to Mid Block and push Wing-Backs to Advanced Wing-Back (IP).
## Pressing Philosophies: High Press, Mid Block, Low Block
Your pressing strategy is a vital part of your overall tactic. FM26 offers three main pressing philosophies:
### High Press
This involves aggressively winning the ball back high up the pitch. It requires players with high Stamina, Work Rate, and Anticipation.
* **OOP Roles:** Pressing CM, Pressing DM, Tracking Winger, Pressing Full-Back, Tracking CF.
* **Team Instruction:** High Press + Pressing Traps.
* **Risk:** Can leave gaps in behind if the press is beaten.
* **Suits:** Teams with energetic squads that want to control the game.
### Mid Block
This involves forming an organized defensive shape in the middle third, allowing the opposition to build up play before pressing in midfield.
* **OOP Roles:** Central Midfielder, Screening DM, Wide-Covering CM, Full-Back.
* **Team Instruction:** Mid Block.
* **Suits:** Balanced teams and counter-attacking setups.
### Low Block
This involves forming a deep defensive shape, absorbing pressure, and hitting the opposition on the counter-attack.
* **OOP Roles:** Covering CB, Holding Full-Back, Holding Wing-Back, Screening DM, Central Outlet CF (stay high for counter).
* **Team Instruction:** Low Block.
* **Suits:** Underdog tactics, protecting a lead, and cup games.
* **Pair With:** Central Outlet CF (OOP) or Splitting Outlet CF (OOP) – stays high as a counter-attack target.
